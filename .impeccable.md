## Design Context

### Users
Customers customizing a dream catcher for themselves. They need approachable choices, a live preview, and a saved design specification.

### Brand Personality
Urban Mynah: calm, earthy, personal, handcrafted home décor. Its supplied logo features a mynah bird, black lettering, an orange U, and the tagline “walls hold stories”. Moon Woven is the fourth design in the Forest Echos collection, not the brand name.

### Brand Assets
- Use the original product photographs and logo extracted from the user's `urbanmynah.pdf` and `logobrand.pdf` for the homepage. Preserve product details; do not generate, retouch, recolor, or replace product photography with 3D renders.
- The user has authorized a realistic image edit of the homepage Moon Woven wall scene: correct scale, hanging support, wall texture, lighting and shadows while preserving the product design and the sage/cream/wood aesthetic. This exception applies to the lifestyle hero; catalog posters and material-detail pages retain their source artwork.
- Lead the homepage with the latest release, currently Moon Woven, and its material details. Follow with the collection title, designs 1–4, then the brand introduction and original first Instagram animation.
- Display the full product in catalog images. User-controlled enlargement and the supplied detail-page slideshow may reveal closer views.
- Preserve the original PDF poster backgrounds in collection cards and enlarged views. Lead the Moon Woven release gallery with the approved realistic edit of its wall-placement scene and keep all four original material-detail pages available.
- The original footer animation autoplays and loops muted by default, with a custom mute/unmute button and no native player controls or caption details.
- Asset provenance lives in `design/urban-mynah-assets.json` and `design/urban-mynah-instagram.json`.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm ivory interface, forest-green accents, a pale sage studio backdrop, elegant serif display type and quiet sans-serif controls. The 3D object is the focal point. This is a design tool, with no implied checkout or order submission.

### Design Principles
- Let customers see the effect of every choice immediately.
- Prioritize the crafted object over decorative interface elements.
- Keep customization approachable and reversible.
- Clearly distinguish a locally saved design from an order.
- Maintain keyboard access, mobile usability, and reduced-motion support.
