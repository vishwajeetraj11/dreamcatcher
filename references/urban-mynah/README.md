# Urban Mynah references

Inspected on 4 October 2026. Sources belong to Urban Mynah; stored here as implementation references.

- [Willow Weave reel](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbqL1rwCApS/): downloaded as `DbqL1rwCApS.mp4`; `willow-contact.jpg` contains sampled video frames. Three overlapping sticks form a triangle, with wrapped jute, painted exposed tips, a fine web, a small dark-red pendant, seven cotton drops, green beads, wooden cylinders, floral diamond charms, and tassels.
- [Sage Haven](https://www.instagram.com/urbanmynah/p/Db8ZwfUknBn/): five intersecting wooden branches, sparse cotton wrapping, ivory dots, a central open web, and one green-bead/wood-bead/clear-quartz drop ending in an amber-colored point. Additional detail photos: `Db_Gd_dkkCt.jpg` and `DcG5J4REiMH.jpg`.
- [Amber Hush photos](https://www.instagram.com/urbanmynah/p/DdEuZY0gsMo/): two long jute-wrapped horizontal sticks, two painted vertical sticks, a square web, triangular suspension, two short side bead chains, and three amber-colored disks above combed cotton leaves. Additional photo: `Dc0zwa1Eh8P.jpg`.
- [Amber Hush reel](https://www.instagram.com/urbanmynah/reel/Dcggb2bi-ci/): downloaded as `Dcggb2bi-ci.mp4`; `amber-contact.jpg` contains sampled frames showing the side profile and leaf fibers.

The studio uses procedural geometry rather than displaying these photos as the 3D models. Stone names describe the modeled appearance; material identity cannot be verified from the photos alone.
